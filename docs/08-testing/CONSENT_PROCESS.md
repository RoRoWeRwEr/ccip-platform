# CCIP Testing Consent Process

## Consent standard

Consent must be informed, specific, understandable, affirmative, documented,
freely given, and withdrawable. Qualified privacy/legal reviewers must approve
the final bilingual notice, lawful basis, wording, storage, retention,
deletion, transfers, age/guardian approach, incentive, and withdrawal effects
before recruitment. This process is not legal advice.

Participation and recording are separate decisions. Declining recording must
not prevent unrecorded participation unless the approved study genuinely
requires it and disclosed that before recruitment. Silence, preselected boxes,
continued browsing, or an NDA signature is not consent.

## Before invitation

1. Approve purpose, minimum data inventory, recipients, systems, locations,
   transfers, retention, deletion, access, incentive, risks, benefits, contacts,
   and withdrawal.
2. Create equivalent Arabic/English notices with version, effective date,
   responsible entity/contact, complaint route, and change process.
3. Approve lawful, non-discriminatory eligibility and age/capacity rules.
4. Configure the restricted consent register and separate identity/code key.
5. Test withdrawal, recording deletion, access revocation, and reconciliation.

## Notice requirements

- responsible entity/contact, purpose, tasks, duration, and accommodations;
- data collected: administration identity/contact, code, consent, observations,
  device/accessibility context, feedback, and separately chosen recording;
- prohibition on real personal/financial inputs;
- purpose/legal basis, recipients/processors, locations/transfers/safeguards;
- retention/deletion and lawful exceptions;
- voluntary nature, incentive, risks/benefits, stopping and refusal effects;
- applicable data rights and complaint routes; and
- NDA/confidentiality terms without removing data rights or protected reports.

## Session sequence

1. Send the approved notice early and allow questions.
2. Verify notice language/version and identity privately.
3. Explain the study, synthetic-data rule, voluntary nature, incentive,
   retention, contacts, and withdrawal.
4. Ask for affirmative **participation** consent; record decision, timestamp,
   version, collector, method, and code in the restricted register.
5. Then ask independently for **recording** consent, naming each actual medium.
6. Start only approved media and show a visible recording indicator.
7. If participation is declined, do not begin or retain more than the approved
   minimal refusal record.
8. If recording is declined, disable it and use the approved unrecorded path.

Use the exact bilingual opening in
[`../PHASE_10_USER_RESEARCH.md`](../PHASE_10_USER_RESEARCH.md); reviewers may
strengthen it while preserving equivalent meaning.

## Ongoing consent and withdrawal

- Remind testers they may pause, skip, or stop at any time.
- Re-consent before a new purpose, data category, recipient, medium, or material
  notice change.
- Accept withdrawal through approved routes; stop collection, revoke access,
  acknowledge, and record scope/time.
- Explain lawful retention limits; do not promise absolute deletion.
- Delete/isolate identifiable artifacts and update derived data where feasible,
  recording completion or an approved exception.

## Short confirmation prompts

These supplement, not replace, the full approved notice.

**English participation:** “Do you voluntarily consent to take part in this
private CCIP study under notice version [version], using only the fictional
scenario provided, knowing that you may stop at any time?”

**English recording:** “Separately, do you consent to [audio/video/screen]
recording for the purposes and retention period stated in the notice? You may
say no to recording.”

**Arabic participation:** «هل توافق طوعاً على المشاركة في دراسة CCIP الخاصة
هذه وفق إشعار الإصدار [رقم الإصدار]، وباستخدام السيناريو الافتراضي المقدم فقط،
مع علمك بأنه يمكنك التوقف في أي وقت؟»

**Arabic recording:** «بشكل منفصل، هل توافق على تسجيل [الصوت/الفيديو/الشاشة]
للأغراض ومدة الاحتفاظ الموضحة في الإشعار؟ يمكنك رفض التسجيل.»

Native-language and legal/privacy reviewers must approve final wording.

## Control failures

Missing evidence, wrong version, bundled/coerced consent, unauthorized
recording, inability to withdraw, or real-data collection is Blocking. Stop,
revoke access, preserve minimal restricted evidence, notify privacy/security,
execute the incident process, and independently retest before resuming.
