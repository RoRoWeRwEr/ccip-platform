# CCIP Testing NDA Requirements Checklist

## Important qualification

This is an operational requirements aid, not legal advice or an NDA. Qualified
Saudi counsel must decide whether an NDA is needed, identify the contracting
entity and governing terms, approve Arabic/English copies, and confirm
electronic-signature validity. Do not use this checklist as legal text.

An NDA does not replace informed consent, a privacy notice, data-processing
terms, acceptable-use rules, or separate recording consent.

## NDA decision record

- [ ] Counsel/owner documented whether each tester type needs an NDA and why.
- [ ] Employees, contractors, agencies, specialists, and public participants
      are classified separately where their relationships differ.
- [ ] Terms are proportionate and preserve security reporting, legal concerns,
      participant-harm reporting, and protected disclosures.
- [ ] Refusal of optional recording or processing is not an NDA breach.
- [ ] If no NDA is required, approved instructions state confidentiality rules.

## Required drafting inputs for counsel

- [ ] Correct legal names, authorized signatories, and notice contacts.
- [ ] Purpose: protected non-production CCIP Preview using synthetic data.
- [ ] Confidential-information definition covering unreleased UI, scenarios,
      invitations, findings, security details, and non-public roadmap content.
- [ ] Exclusions for lawfully public, previously known, independently developed,
      lawfully received, and legally compelled information.
- [ ] Permitted-use limits; no sharing, posting, scraping, reverse engineering,
      or action outside authorized test scope.
- [ ] Safeguards, approved devices/channels, incident reporting, and onward-
      disclosure limits.
- [ ] Proportionate term and confidentiality duration.
- [ ] Return/deletion aligned with retention, legal holds, backups, and
      research withdrawal.
- [ ] Feedback ownership/licensing and protection for pre-existing materials.
- [ ] No promise of error-free, regulated, approved, or production-ready status.
- [ ] Remedies, liability, law, jurisdiction, notices, assignment,
      severability, amendment, and entire-agreement terms reviewed.
- [ ] Arabic/English legal-language review and precedence rule.
- [ ] Electronic signature, capacity, age/guardian, and copy-delivery rules.

## Execution checklist

- [ ] Approved version/effective date locked before invitation.
- [ ] Tester has time to review and ask questions.
- [ ] No blank fields, hidden exhibits, or post-signature changes.
- [ ] Signer identity/authority verified through the approved process.
- [ ] Required parties sign before confidential access.
- [ ] Each party receives an executed copy.
- [ ] Contract/signature/identity stay in the restricted legal system, not Git.
- [ ] Access record contains only status, version, date, and restricted reference.
- [ ] Expiry, renewal, and deletion reminders are scheduled.
