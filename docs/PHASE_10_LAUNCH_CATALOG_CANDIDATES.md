## Selection rationale

The proposed acceptance set is 12 currently listed credit cards from three
Saudi issuers: four general/rewards, cashback, premium, or travel products per
issuer. The set is intentionally small enough for line-by-line bilingual
review while covering different networks, fee levels, reward models, and
eligibility segments.

“Effective date” below means an exact date or bounded date range visibly shown
by an official source for the material pricing/terms. A URL filename containing
`2026`, a search-engine publication date, or the research access date does not
qualify.

## Independent-review checklist

`INDEPENDENT-BILINGUAL-REVIEWER-01` must:

1. Open every English and Arabic official source directly and confirm issuer
   ownership, current availability, source-access date, and exact product name.
2. Reconcile product pages with the latest official fee schedule and terms;
   treat promotions separately from permanent fees and confirm VAT treatment.
3. Validate every exact effective date. Do not accept URL years, metadata,
   search snippets, or retrieval dates as effective dates.
4. Check annual/supplementary/replacement/foreign/cash fees, monthly rate, APR,
   minimum payment, and material eligibility without inferring missing facts.
5. Check earn rates, caps, minimums, exclusions, expiry, valuation basis,
   network benefits, insurance, lounge conditions, and campaign end dates.
6. Resolve the recorded Riyad Cashback lounge conflict, Al Rajhi post-promotion
   annual fees, SNB Cashback cash-advance wording, and all missing dated terms.
7. Confirm Arabic/English material meaning and record any mistranslation or
   issuer inconsistency as an exception, never silently normalize it.
8. Sign only with the reviewer role code, date, result, and redacted evidence
   reference. Personal details must remain outside the repository and chat.

No record may be written to private staging until the owner approves a revised
list and the independent review is complete. This inventory does not close the
catalog gate or Phase 10.
