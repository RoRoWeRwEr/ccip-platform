## Current critical-path risk

P9.2 is complete: separate protected staging projects, scoped authorization and
values, `fra1`, exact-commit deployment, health/readiness, bilingual UI, logs,
and dependency security were verified. P9.3 is the current critical path and
still requires an independently owner-verified staging-administrator identity
for the bootstrap exercise plus operational alert/rollback evidence.
Production launch remains conditional on qualified Saudi legal/privacy review.

## Escalation rules

- Any suspected privacy breach, secret exposure, cross-user/cross-bank access,
  guaranteed-outcome claim, discriminatory ranking defect, or unauthorized
  publication is Critical: stop the affected release path, preserve evidence,
  and escalate to the owner/security lead.
- Risks involving legal position, personal-data policy, paid vendors, hosting
  regions, monetization, or regulated-advice boundaries require owner input
  under `docs/AUTONOMOUS_DECISION_POLICY.md`.
- Closing a risk requires repository or operational evidence and a dated log
  entry; a plan or passing unrelated CI run is not closure evidence.
